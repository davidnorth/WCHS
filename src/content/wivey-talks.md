# Wivey Talks

All talks are at St Andre's Church, Wiveliscombe and start at 7:30pm. Doors open 7:00pm.

Admission free to members with annual subscriptions of £12 per person or £16:00 for two people at the same address. Visitors £5


## Youth, community and the Power of Being
17th September 2026
With Nick Harvey - charity manager, In the Mix
What youth work is, what is means and supporting young people to thrive.

## Positive Planning for Property Development
22nd October 2026
With Rebecca Randall - Chartered Planner, Polden Planning
Rebecca consders the challenges and opportunities of a new housing and employment in thearea and how the community can work with the planning system
to achieve positive change and local benefits.


## The Tudor Doomesday Book
19th November 2026
With Professor James Clark - Exeter University
Prof. Clark discusses Henry VIII's survey of 16th century England & Wales
and the unique view it offers of the Tudor West Country.


## Wiveliscombe Brewing --tag Christmas special--
17th December 2026
With Bob Croft - Archaeologist & Simon French - Exmoor Ales
Bob & Simon review Wivey's brewing history and the challenges facing modern brewers. Exmoor ales will be available.


## The Secrets of Abbotsfield
21st January 2027
With Julian White - Author, Abbotsfield Residend
This historic building is home to a thriving residential community and has a fascinating history. Julian dips into his book on the subject.

## Exmoor National Park, past, present and future
18th February 2027
With Rob Wilson-North - Enpa
What is Exmoor and what is its future? Rob leads us through the rolercoaster story of 
Exmoor over 8000 years and anticipates possible challenges.


## East Quay, Watchet. Building a cultural center in a coastal town
18th Marth 2027
With Naomi Griffith - Director, Onion Collective
Naomi describes the long journey towards creating and operating East Quay.

## Sgt. Harry Brett - Wartime Stories
15th April 2027
With Marion Pollinger
Marion shares her grandfather's memories of a life in a German prisoner of war camp during WWII. Pictures and recordings of him bring his stories to life.


## Discovering Churches in West Somerset
20th May 2027
With Tom Mayberry
Tom guides us through the rich diversity of medieval and later parish churches in one of the West Country's most memorable regions.
